## Creative observations

Capy couples warm comic-driven mascot illustration with comparatively sober UI, using consistent line quality and recurring contextual props. Treat this as a high-level principle, not a compositional template.
Transfer: character pauses at boundaries and helps explain states. Do not copy the capybara silhouette, eyewear, branded scenes, color blocking, or proprietary layouts.
Motion observed below is limited to DOM-computed transition/animation candidates and screenshot states; not all scroll-triggered motion has been individually verified.
Accessibility check: maintain meaningful status labels independent of illustrations, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior and legible small sizes.

## Expanded art-direction observations by page family (manual review of local browser captures)

| Page family | Observed example | Visual hierarchy and illustration use | Transferable rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | / | Condensed tall heading, monospace explanatory copy, black top bar, large live-product surface underneath. Illustration is secondary to the interface. | Let actual product concepts establish credibility; Rovi emphasizes key lines rather than replacing proof. |
| Automation | /features/automations | Large left headline with small character in upper action area, followed by a large product screenshot. | Character moments should not compete with controls. |
| Integration | /integrations/github | Narrow copy column to left, framed art to right, section headline under a capsule label. | Use modular editorial panels, while staying visually distinct from Capy. |
| Pricing | /pricing | Large character sitting on the lower boundary of the first section, interacting with a playful ring; strong border below and clear CTA. | Characters can acknowledge UI borders. Do not imitate the ring/pose or identifiable staging. |
| Security | /security | Independent outlined technical illustration inside a framed right-hand panel. | Treat uncertain/technical subjects with restraint and literal labels. |
| About | /about | Typography-first, black hairline separators, small section icon, large white zones. | Editorial hierarchy can carry the narrative even with few illustrations. |
| Blog/articles/changelog | /blog, /articles, /changelog | Repeated article templates with content-heavy cards and limited mascot prominence. | Scale character appearances down on reference material. |
| Legal | /privacy-policy, /terms-of-service, etc. | Long text with little ornament. | Do not clutter legal/operational content with a decorative cast. |

**Typography sampled:** heading family is bebasNeue; body family is abcSocialMono on sampled public pages. These are reference observations, not OpenReview font dependencies or files to redistribute.

**Visual system observations:** Capy favors thin, high-contrast black borders, mostly white backgrounds, extremely bold compressed headlines, short monospace supporting copy, mint action accents, and occasional rounded comic character moments. Its placement of the mascot near actual product screenshots and horizontal section dividers is instructive. Rovi instead uses a folded-page silhouette, integrated lens face, warm-paper editorial workspaces, subdued ink, and distinct verification-state language.

**Interaction limits:** Playwright captured both responsive viewport sizes for all 82 sitemap routes and intermediate scroll positions for the key landing/page families. It also captured homepage navigation hover samples. A candidate transition/animation inventory was recorded per route in capy_inventory.json. That evidence does not establish exact keyframe durations, every hovered state, every sticky transition, or each infinite-scroll interaction. No such unobserved behavior is claimed.

**Additional public links discovered:** 10 routes linked from the download page include macOS/Linux/Windows binary downloads and one RSS feed. These are not unique marketing HTML layouts and were not downloaded or executed. See capy_inventory.json for the exact URLs.

**Documentation:** 153 docs HTML route URLs were inventoried from the separate docs sitemap (306 XML entries including OG images). Those routes are recorded, not misrepresented as 153 individually inspected browser layouts. Documentation visual behavior requires follow-up for a full docs audit.

**External reference:** Contra was re-scrolled to force lazy-load images; 26/26 DOM images reported nonzero natural dimensions and eight loaded-page screenshot positions were added. The Patty launch-video exact URL returned HTTP 404; only verified public textual summaries, not actual frames, inform the motion principles.
